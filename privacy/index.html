PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: April 2026

WHO WE ARE

La Table Marrakech is a private chef service operating in Marrakech, Morocco. Contact: hello@latablemarrakech.com

WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

When you submit a booking request, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Preferred date, time, and number of guests
  • Dietary restrictions or special requests

This information is sent directly to our WhatsApp business number to process your booking. We do not store this data on any server.

HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We use your information solely to:

  • Confirm and coordinate your private chef booking
  • Communicate about your reservation via WhatsApp or email
  • Accommodate your dietary needs

COOKIES

This website uses minimal cookies:

  • Language preference (localStorage) — to remember your selected language (EN/FR/AR)
  • Cookie consent (localStorage) — to remember your cookie preference

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Ads conversion tracking to improve our service. These cookies only activate after you accept them via our cookie banner. Pixel data is processed by Google in line with their privacy practices. If enabled in the future, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel may be used to understand site traffic.

THIRD PARTIES

Your booking data is transmitted via WhatsApp (operated by Meta). We use Google Fonts for typography. No data is sold to third parties.

YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold. Contact us at hello@latablemarrakech.com for any privacy-related requests.

DATA RETENTION

We retain booking communications for up to 12 months for service quality purposes, then delete them.
