Where we cook

We’re based in the Marrakech medina. We work across the city and out to:

  • Marrakech proper — Palmeraie, Hivernage, Gueliz, Agdal, the medina. No transport charge.
  • Atlas valleys — Ourika, Ouirgane, Imlil. Flat €20–40 transport add-on.
  • Agafay desert — for sunset and overnight camp dinners. Flat €60 per person transport.
  • Essaouira / Atlantic coast — on request, for events of 10+ guests.

Hours & response time

The chef takes bookings every day. Service nights happen any night of the week (Friday royal couscous nights are popular — book those further ahead). WhatsApp replies typically within an hour during 9am–9pm Morocco time (GMT+1), longer at night.

Last-minute bookings under 48 hours: message us with the date and we’ll let you know if a chef is free that night.
