Why we exist

Restaurants are loud.
Decisions need quiet.

The dinners that move a deal forward are not the dinners with a wine list as long as a passport. They are the ones where the room is yours, the chef is in the kitchen, and the table is small enough that every voice carries. That is what we cook.

Founders bring their boards. PE firms bring their portfolio CEOs. Tech offsites bring their leadership team. The brief is always the same: we want this to feel like Marrakech — not like a hotel ballroom.

We meet your EA on email or WhatsApp, we send a quote in 24 hours, we invoice in EUR or USD, and we handle the venue if you don't have one (riad, villa, palmeraie, or an Agafay desert camp at sunset). Your team shows up. We do the rest.

Multilingual service. NDAs available. Discreet by default. We have cooked for groups whose names you'd recognise; we don't post about it.

FAQ

Asked by every
EA

How much does corporate dining cost in Marrakech?

From €650 for 10 guests, scaling per guest. Includes menu design, souk sourcing, full service team and cleanup. No per-guest service charges, no minimum bar spend.

Do you provide an invoice?

Yes. EUR or USD, VAT-compliant, suitable for EU/UK/US bookkeeping. Net-30 available for repeat clients. Stripe, wire or company card all accepted.

Can you handle a venue if we don't have one?

Yes. We work with a vetted set of riads, villas and desert camps. Tell us the group size, the budget envelope, and the night, and we'll come back with two or three options that match.

Are NDAs available?

Yes — mutual or one-way, signed before the quote if you prefer. Discretion is the default; we don't post about clients.
