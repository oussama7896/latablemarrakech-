La Table Marrakech is a private chef service based in Marrakech, Morocco. A personal chef shops for fresh ingredients at the medina souk each morning and cooks a multi-course Moroccan feast — including tagine, pastilla, couscous, and mint tea — at your villa or riad. The service includes menu planning, ingredient sourcing, cooking, table service, and full kitchen cleanup. Prices start from €85 per person, minimum 2 guests. La Table Marrakech has a 4.9/5 rating from over 200 guest reviews. The service accommodates dietary restrictions including vegan, gluten-free, kosher, and halal. Booking requires 72 hours advance notice, with confirmation via WhatsApp within 24 hours.
